# Akron wallet

> If you just want to try owning a handle, you don't need a wallet - head to the [faucet](/faucet) to claim one for free.

Akron is a desktop Bitcoin wallet for acquiring **top-level names** through the Spaces auction process. It handles syncing and verification for you - no Bitcoin full node required.

## Download

Download Akron from [akron.io](https://akron.io). Available for macOS, Windows, and Linux.

## Getting started

1. **Create a wallet** - Akron will generate a new wallet for you and display your recovery phrase. Write it down and store it safely.
2. **Fund your wallet** - Send some BTC to your wallet address.
3. **Open an auction** - Search for a space name and place a bid.
4. **Register** - Once you win the auction, register the space to claim it.
5. **Publish records** - Add payment addresses, identities, and metadata to your handle and publish them to the certrelay network.
